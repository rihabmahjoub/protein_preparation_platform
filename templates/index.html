Step 3 — Structure preparation (PDBFixer)
What PDBFixer does: Removes solvent molecules and heterogens (ligands, ions), reconstructs missing residues and heavy atoms using a force-field template, then adds hydrogen atoms at the specified pH. The resulting structure is ready for protonation review and PDBQT conversion.
Ref: Eastman P et al. (2013) J Chem Theory Comput 9:461–469.

Step 4 — Protonation state analysis
What this step does: PDBFixer already added hydrogens at the target pH (Step 3). This step verifies those assignments using the Henderson-Hasselbalch equation and identifies residues where the protonation choice is ambiguous — i.e., where |pH − pKa| < 2 units. This does not modify the structure; it guides you on which residues to check manually.

Why it matters for docking: Histidine tautomers (HID/HIE/HIP) and cysteine protonation state can change hydrogen-bond patterns in the binding site and significantly alter docking scores. Ignoring this is a common source of error in published docking studies.

Figures explained: Left — count of each titratable residue type. Right — fraction protonated at the target pH for ambiguous residues (red = mostly protonated, blue = mostly deprotonated). Residues close to 0.5 are most uncertain.

Step 5 — PDBQT conversion (AutoDock Vina receptor)
What PDBQT is: The format required by AutoDock Vina for the rigid receptor. Only heavy atoms and polar hydrogens (bonded to N, O, S) are retained. AutoDock 4 atom types are assigned. Vina's own scoring function does not use receptor partial charges, so placeholder values (0.000) are written.
Ref: Trott O & Olson AJ (2010) J Comput Chem 31:455–461.
Step 6 — Binding site prediction
How it works: The protein is mapped onto a 3D grid. Voxels occupied by protein atoms (VDW + probe radius) are marked. All remaining free voxels connected to the grid boundary are labelled as exterior solvent. Any enclosed free cavity is a candidate pocket, ranked by druggability score.

Druggability score (0–1, Halgren 2009): combines pocket volume (optimal ~700 Å³), hydrophobic content (>50% favours drug binding), and buriedness. Scores above 0.5 suggest a potentially druggable site.

Lining residues highlighted in red on the viewer give the coordinates for the AutoDock Vina grid box.
